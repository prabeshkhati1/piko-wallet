# Piko Wallet branding

Original vector mark: a rounded violet tile, a white P, and a mint wallet clasp. The purple and green connect it to Ivy Wallet's visual palette while identifying the app as Piko.

The logo was authored directly as SVG. No image-generation model or CLI was used: that capability was unavailable in this session. Raster installation icons are deterministic exports of this vector.

Asset brief: “A minimal, friendly Piko Wallet app mark; a white P shaped like a wallet on a rounded violet tile, with a small mint clasp; crisp geometry, recognizable at small sizes, no additional text.”

Source: `static/icons/icon.svg`. Raster exports include 192 px, 512 px, a 512 px maskable icon, and a 180 px Apple touch icon. The icon's main mark fits the central maskable safe area.
